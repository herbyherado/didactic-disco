The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first books 12 May and gets an invite for 5 December. Any channel: app, email, and the booking-v2 form, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY. This is the bug #4471 names, and the fix queued for it.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run booking-v3. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports through the form. The parser fix does not touch it; the hint, or the form’s reading of it, has to change.

shift: the date moves by one day. No month and day swap. A Tuesday invite lands on the Monday, one meeting reads Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, an invite arrives before the booking. That is a time zone, not a date format. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and then filed them under parser by tenant; here they get their own ticket.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merged on subject. Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. People write the same few subjects whatever went wrong, so subject lines cannot keep these problems apart: the table above counts the ones they share.
  • Tagged by browser. Intake guesses the cause from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which form they run (locales.ts). The notes under the table count where the two disagree.
  • Counted once. “Merge early, count once” counted reports, not causes.

What the desk does now

  1. New date reports: place them with problemOf (#lib/kb/problems), by tenant, channel and desk notes, not by subject or browser. A report it cannot place goes to a person, not into a ticket.
  2. Read the body when the dates in it are not a month and day swap. That is how shift was found.
  3. Decision 0001 stays as accepted; a new record should replace it with “same cause, same ticket”.
